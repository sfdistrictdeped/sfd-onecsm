Your feedback matters to us. Help us improve the quality, efficiency, and responsiveness of our public services through your participation in the Client Satisfaction Measurement.

✓ Data Privacy Notice

The San Fernando District Office collects information through this system to process your Certificate of Appearance and administer the Client Satisfaction Measurement (CSM).

Information submitted through this system will be used for these purposes and handled by authorized personnel in accordance with applicable data protection requirements.

Participation in the satisfaction survey is voluntary. You may leave optional questions unanswered. Your feedback will help the office assess and improve its services.

Before this system is placed into public service, the office's official privacy contact, data-retention period, and information about your data-subject rights must be provided in the approved privacy notice.

Please read the notice and provide your consent to continue.
